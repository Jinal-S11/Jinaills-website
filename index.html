Pricing & sizing

Every set includes: 10 hand-shaped press-ons, nail glue tabs, a cuticle stick, and a mini file.

Shipping: ships within 5–7 days of order — each set is made after you order, not before.

Sizing: use the size guide in the order form below, or send me your nail widths in mm and I'll match them.

Sizing guide

No measuring tape? A ruler works. Measure the width of each nail bed edge-to-edge in millimeters.

  • Extra small7–8mm
  • Small9–10mm
  • Medium11–13mm
  • Large14–16mm
  • Extra large17mm+

Orders confirm by email once submitted. Payment details are sent after your sizing is reviewed.
